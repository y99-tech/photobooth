🖼️ Custom frame overlay

Transparent PNG drawn on top of every photo (e.g. from Canva, 3:2 for DSLR, 16:9 for webcam). Set template to "none" if the frame has its own text.

🟩 Green screen backgrounds

Turn it on in the Green screen tab. Put a green (or blue) backdrop behind guests, light it evenly, and keep guests a step or two in front of it. Upload your own backgrounds (JPEG/PNG, landscape 3:2 or 16:9) — 4 built-in ones are always available.

🖨️ Printer

Turn printing on and choose paper size, copies and auto-print in the Printing tab. Leave printer empty to use the system default.
